### Semantic bridges and obligations

| Bridge | Audit obligations |
|---|---|

| Serialized artifact → UPLC term | Check the precise Flat/CBOR decoding route, language-version selection, and rejection of malformed or trailing input. Compare decoded structure against the pinned artifact. |
| Ledger inputs → Plutus `Data` | Check datum, redeemer, and context encodings, including constructor tags, field order, integer and byte encodings, and partial or failed decoding. |
| Original term → prepared proposition | Account for input application and every preprocessing or simplification step. Prove preservation of the stated outcome, or state and prove the property over the original term. |
| Lean evaluator → reference semantics | Compare evaluation order, error behavior, reachable builtins and version-specific semantics. Check evaluation-cost accounting separately from ledger CPU and memory budgets. |
| Lean context → ledger context | Compare the selected era’s context fields and validity predicates against the applicable ledger rules; establish that assumptions admit intended ledger-valid contexts. |
| Proof → assurance claim | Preserve the exact theorem, definitions, assumptions, and bounds. Record Lean kernel acceptance separately from exported replay, independent-kernel acceptance, evaluator conformance, and correspondence to Cardano execution. |

For builtins, inventory those reachable from the pinned script and audit their definitions, edge cases, and costs. Identify cryptographic primitives and whether their results rely on axioms, external implementations, or unchecked assumptions. Differential tests and published vectors can provide evidence, but do not establish universal correctness.

### Outcomes and non-vacuity

Keep decoding failure, evaluation failure, fuel or budget exhaustion, and successful evaluation as distinct outcomes. Exhaustion is incomplete, not script failure. A counterexample or witness should include its decoded inputs and a replayable evaluation trace.

For non-vacuity, require: a valid successful sale; a valid failing sale; an intentionally invalid context rejected by the stated validity premise; and a replayable, ledger-valid multi-satisfaction attack witness. Check that the assumptions are jointly satisfiable and that the attack witness reaches the relevant branch.
