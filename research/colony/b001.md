### Scoring and comparison protocol

Score **solver success** and **certified proof success** independently:

- **Solver success:** tool reports the intended result within fixed limits. Record solver/version, timeout, resource limits, and result. This is not proof certification.
- **Certified proof success:** the original full contract is proved, with original definitions, assumptions, and bounds preserved, then accepted by the stated checker. Report the exact assurance level for each result; do not equate solver output or elaborator acceptance with exported replay or independent-kernel acceptance.
- Score per-family counts and macro-average family success so large families cannot dominate. Report positives, invalid/negative cases, timeouts, unsupported cases, and infrastructure errors separately.
- Do not key evaluator logic or scoring on benchmark names. Use manifest fields such as task type, expected outcome, source digest, contract, and checker configuration.
- Compare on the same toolchain and checker configuration when possible. Lean Blaster’s own Lean/solver stack and SV-COMP verifier toolchains are not the same as this repository’s Lean 4.35.0 evaluator; absent a verified common setup, mark those comparisons **incompatible** rather than rank them directly.
