## Proposed role charters

Proposals require separate controller authorization; this audit grants no permissions.

- Research scoper — Purpose: prevent oversized investigations from consuming attempts without artifacts. Owner: researcher; existing read-only researcher execution profile. Responsibilities: select one question, a finite source list, one deliverable, numeric output limits and a stopping rule; retain negative results. Handoff: independently reviewed brief to an existing researcher or engineer. Cannot change theorem statements, acceptance gates, budgets or permissions.

- Review evidence curator — Purpose: make an exact candidate reviewable before a reviewer call begins. Owner: researcher; existing read-only researcher profile. Responsibilities: inventory exact base/head, accessible diff and sources, dated task/PR/CI evidence and missing information; distinguish parked cycles from active reviews. Handoff: evidence packet to the independent reviewer through the controller. Does not author the verdict, contact reviewers or publish independently.

- Dispatch-fit steward — Purpose: prevent implementation jobs from repeatedly reaching incompatible sandboxes or publication scopes. Owner: sre; existing fixed-allowlist SRE governance profile. Responsibilities: map deliverables to current authority, propose explicit blocked reasons and the smallest viable decomposition, identify historical role aliases requiring governance decisions. Handoff: separate governance proposal to independent review, CI and controller integration. No role installation, permission grant, merge or deployment authority.
