## Controls and reporting required for every admission

Before a family or shard is admitted, its artifact should bind the exact upstream commit, source file hashes, selected target identities, unchanged statement/assumption/bound hashes, preserved proof-free context and imports, source/configuration selector, and resource profile. Each scored target’s **entire proof body must be fixed to exactly `by colony`**. No manual proof fragments, helper target proofs, or upstream proof bodies may contribute to the tactic score.

Each admission needs positive non-vacuity witnesses and intentionally invalid controls appropriate to the family. Keep controls separate from canonical target counts. Report every target, including timeouts, errors, source/admission blocks, failures, and unattempted slots; missing evidence cannot become success or progress. Keep resource limits identical across compared strategies and disclose whether they match upstream. For Home and UPLC cases, audit possible duplicate mathematics or source-slot overlap, while preserving canonical slots unless a separately governed corpus revision changes them.
