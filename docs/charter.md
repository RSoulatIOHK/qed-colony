# Project charter

QED Colony exists to build a powerful local Lean 4 tactic for program verification through an autonomous, publicly auditable research process. The long-term flagship is correctness properties of complex compiled Cardano contracts.

## Fixed outcomes, open architecture

The swarm may replace every part of the pilot proof engine. It may investigate symbolic execution, program logics, induction, reflection, solver certificates, lemma discovery, or new combinations. Blaster is a baseline. Existing Cardano formalizations are starting material; optimized forks need explicit semantic justification.

The shipped tactic uses no runtime LLM. Research agents may use models while developing reusable procedures and proving supporting lemmas. Every claimed theorem must connect back to its prescribed statement under a documented standard-axiom policy.

## Research governance

The initial organization has one coordinator and a bounded pool of temporary specialists. Tasks have an owner, dependencies, acceptance criteria, an execution budget, artifacts, and a recorded conclusion. A negative experiment is useful evidence. Managers must not create unbounded recursive management structures.

All durable changes go through public PRs. Consequential local decisions become public experiment notes. Independent reviews cover semantics, trust, performance, and maintainability as appropriate. An agent cannot satisfy acceptance by changing its own challenge, permitted axioms, resource limit, or evaluator.

The controller may automate ordinary merges once a separately deployed acceptance policy exists. The bootstrap does not yet possess that external enforcement and therefore does not enable automatic merges.

## Human role

The owner supplies objectives, research taste, and resource constraints. The owner need not write implementation code. Scope changes and new deployment or spending authority remain visible decisions. A successful worker process is a review candidate, not a proof or a merged result.

## Public claims

Publish measured results with exact revisions, toolchains, resource limits, failures, and trust categories. Do not call a toy marketplace model a proof of an on-chain script. Do not call elaborator acceptance independent replay. Do not call a public development corpus held out.
